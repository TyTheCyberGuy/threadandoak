Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before your first pickup.

What areas do you serve?

We are currently launching in select Morrisville neighborhoods: Kitts Creek, Keystone Crossing, Townes at Everett Crossing, Providence Place, and Mason Farms by Ashton Woods. If your neighborhood is not listed, submit your neighborhood on the order form or text us at (919) 435-4608.

How does pickup work?

Select your neighborhood route, leave your laundry bag at your door during your pickup window, and we take it from there. You do not need to be home.

What detergent do you use?

We use premium detergent and offer a fragrance-free option for sensitive skin. If you have a preference or allergy, add it to your order notes and we will accommodate it whenever possible.

What if my neighborhood is not listed?

Choose “My neighborhood is not listed” on the order form and tell us your neighborhood and ZIP code. We will use those requests to decide where to expand next.

Do you handle delicates?

Not at this time. Thread & Oak currently focuses on everyday wash-and-fold laundry only. Please do not include dry-clean-only, delicate, hang-dry, or irreplaceable items in your laundry bag.

How do I pay?

We accept major payment methods listed during scheduling. You will see the order details clearly, and there are no tipping prompts.

Ready to get your weekends back?

Start with a one-time order. No subscription required.
