Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before your first pickup.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve Cary and Morrisville — including neighborhoods like Amberly, Preston, Carpenter Village, Graystone, Kitts Creek, and nearby areas. Not sure if we cover your address? Send us a note.

How does pickup work?

Schedule online, leave your bag at the door during your pickup window, and we take it from there. No need to be home.

What detergent do you use?

We use premium detergent and offer a fragrance-free option for sensitive skin. If you have a preference or allergy, add it to your order notes and we'll accommodate it whenever possible.

What if something is damaged?

We treat every order with care. If something goes wrong, reach out right away and we will review it with you and make it right whenever possible.

Do you handle delicates?

Yes. Delicates and hang-dry items can be handled by request. Just flag them in your order notes and separate them if needed.

How do I pay?

We accept major payment methods listed during scheduling. You will see the order details clearly, and there are no tipping prompts.

Ready to get your weekends back?

Start with a one-time order. No subscription required.
